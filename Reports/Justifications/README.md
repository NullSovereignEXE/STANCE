# Justifications and Notes

This directory will be used to record notes on the justification for choices made in our code, along with additional supporting information and background material.

It may include:
- Explanations for design and implementation decisions
- Notes on assumptions, trade-offs, and validation choices
- Relevant physics concepts and technical background
- Graphs and figures to support our analysis and reports
- Extra information useful when writing project reports

The goal is to keep all reasoning, supporting details, and reference material organized in one place for easier documentation and reporting.
